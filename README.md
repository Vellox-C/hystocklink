# HyStockLink

A Fabric mod that lets you switch Minecraft accounts from the title screen without restarting the game.

A widget sits on the title screen. Paste your account token in, hit Activate, and your session switches instantly.

**Tokens are available at [hystock.shop](https://hystock.shop).**

## Installation

1. Install [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) for your MC version.
2. Download the JAR for your version from [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/hystocklink/versions) or the [releases page](https://github.com/Vellox-C/hystocklink/releases).
3. Drop it into your `mods/` folder.

## Usage

1. Click **Activate Account** on the widget.
2. Paste your token and confirm.
3. Connect to any server as that account.

Hold **Left Ctrl** and drag to reposition the widget. Position saves automatically.

The 26.1 JAR covers 26.1, 26.1.1, and 26.1.2.
